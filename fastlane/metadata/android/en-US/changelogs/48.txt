- Adapt to volume now uses the ISO 226 equal-loudness contours, with a live graph and a usual volume for each pair of headphones
- The limiter has attack, release, ratio and post-gain, with one-tap styles, and can never be set to clip
- New Bass character: Natural, Punch or Sustain
- The Sound tab is tidier: less-used sections fold away and show what's on while closed
- Edits to a preset are kept when you switch to another one and back, until the app closes, with Discard edits to drop them
- A bar on the EQ page offers Save and Discard as soon as a preset has changes
- A fader at the top no longer clashes with the overview above it
- The opened status strip says what each effect is doing, such as the reverb room and which apps it reaches
- Adapt to volume keeps its strength per output, like its usual volume
- Fixed the preset switcher sometimes leaving out presets, such as while Flat was in use
- Double tap a fader or a preset setting to return it to the preset's saved value
- Two presets can no longer share a name
- Sound now only holds what's saved with the preset. Settings for every preset, such as the limiter, night mode, balance and ear correction, moved to Settings (formerly System)
