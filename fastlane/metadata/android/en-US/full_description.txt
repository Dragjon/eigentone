Phonons is a free equaliser that changes the sound of apps across your phone, including Spotify and YouTube, without root. No ads, no account and no tracking.

Find your sound
- Correct your headphones using AutoEq's published measurements, offered when they connect
- A guided A/B listening test, where the result has to beat what you already have
- Compare any two sounds instantly at matched loudness, with an optional blind test
- A hearing check that builds a gentle curve for how you hear, with a separate EQ for each ear if they differ (not a medical test)

Shape it yourself
- Graphic EQ with 10, 15 or 31 faders, and draw a curve with one sweep
- Parametric EQ with up to 20 filters
- 595 searchable shapes by genre, instrument, situation or problem
- Hear a shape on your own music before keeping it, and layer shapes together
- Type in the EQ from another app to make your own shape
- Import and export GraphicEQ files at full detail, and import ParametricEQ files

Made for daily use
- Presets that load automatically for each output, earbud noise mode and app, and change with a smooth morph
- A free three-band compressor with adjustable crossovers, a response curve and starting styles
- Volume leveller, scheduled night mode, loudness compensation and limiter
- Balance and virtual surround
- Share a sound with a text code
- Quick Settings tile and home-screen widget

Good to know
- EQ in a headphone app adds to Phonons, so it's best set to flat
- Android allows this effect on most apps, but it varies by phone
- Reverb and head tracking aren't offered, because Android doesn't allow them to work reliably without root
