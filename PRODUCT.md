# Product

## Platform

android

## Users

Headphone enthusiasts on Android phones who know AutoEq, Q and dB, and want precise control over how their headphones sound. They use it around music: usually to switch presets, adjust the faders or curve, or check that the EQ is working, then they leave. Tools and tests are occasional.

## Product Purpose

A free, system-wide equaliser for Android 13 and newer that needs no root. It works with Spotify, YouTube and most other apps, and tunes sound to one person's ears and headphones. Success is sound the listener prefers, confirmed by their own ears rather than by the app's claims.

## Positioning

Tuned to your ears. "Eigen" means "own", so Eigentone reads as "your own tone". Its hearing check, per-ear correction, guided peak finder and blind A/B comparison let a listener measure their own preferences on their own headphones, alongside published AutoEq corrections.

## Operating Context

Used on a phone with headphones on, usually while music plays in another app, often in short visits and in any lighting. Android's audio effects vary by phone. The engine reports what the phone accepted, and it can't report live levels or compressor gain reduction from inside the effect.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Graphic EQ (10, 15 or 31 faders), parametric EQ, and a shape library of 350 curves, plus the user's own shapes.
- AutoEq corrections with measurement comparison and a tuner, GraphicEQ and ParametricEQ files.
- Hearing check with per-ear correction, guided peak finder, frequency sweep, Find my sound, and blind A/B comparison.
- Presets per output, noise mode and app, with search, folders, share codes, backups, a Quick Settings tile, a widget and app icon shortcuts.
- Compressor, harshness and boom tamers, volume leveller, scheduled night mode, loudness compensation, limiter, balance, stereo width, optional virtual surround, and reverb on apps that announce their audio.
- Android doesn't allow channel mixing, crossfeed or head tracking here. Nothing may be drawn as a live measurement unless it really is one.
- Internal app ID and class names still say Phonons until the first public release.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Eigentone.
- Free, with no ads, no account and no tracking. Crash reports stay on the phone.
- Plain, honest explanations of every setting, including what it can't do.

## Evidence on Hand

No testimonials, user numbers or independent reviews exist, and none may be invented. Only tested on a Galaxy A54 running Android 16.

## Product Principles

- Your ears decide. Offer the comparison, and don't declare a sound correct.
- Show only what's true, and say so when something is an estimate.
- Precision in reach: enthusiasts shouldn't have to dig for a control.
- Fast in and out for the daily tasks: switching presets, adjusting, and checking.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Controls have a screen reader alternative to gestures, such as band buttons beside the faders. Colour is never the only signal.
