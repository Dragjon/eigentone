Scope: the whole Eigentone app. Mode: Operate.

Audience and job: headphone enthusiasts switching presets, adjusting the curve and checking the EQ works, in short visits with music playing. Tools and tests are occasional.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Eigentone is the instrument that measures and tunes your hearing, as a precision tool you own. It refuses the category's default arrangement of a glossy dark app with coloured pill sliders and rounded cards.

OWN-WORLD: Graphite enamel faceplate, lit ivory push-keys, engraved condensed labels, fine graph grid, steady tabular readouts. Left ear blue, right ear red with audiogram symbols, the only chroma. Ivory means lit or active. Danger is orange so it can't be confused with the right ear. Corners are small and square-shouldered. Height is the only hierarchy: faceplate, raised key, pressed key.

STORY: On opening, a listener sees what's connected, what's running and the curve, can switch preset in one tap, and can adjust straight away. Occasional tests live together in their own place.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Title and power at the top, a status strip beneath, then the graph on its fine grid, a row of lit preset keys, then the fader bank with rectangular caps and engraved legends. Bottom navigation is the only other chrome.

FORM: The Audiometer, candidate 5 of 7 on the ordered list, seed key 44462acb. Raises: from Labanotation, left and right are mirrored around a centre line with the left ear on the left; from the Acetate Manual, surface height is the only hierarchy.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: audiogram markers, X for left and O for right, on every per-ear graph, so a person's own hearing is drawn the way an audiologist would.

Unresolved: dark only for now, with no light theme.
